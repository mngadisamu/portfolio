## Overview

**Tower** is an AI-powered complaint triage assistant for airline customer service — my Week 4 individual AI Solution project on the CAPACITI x Clickatell AI Bootcamp.

## The problem

Airline support teams receive a high volume of customer complaints across channels, and working out which ones are urgent, what they're about and who should handle them is slow when done by hand.

## The solution

A working prototype with a responsive AI that reads incoming complaints and helps the team triage them, built on the Kaggle US airline tweets dataset and deployed on Streamlit Community Cloud.

## Built with

Python · Streamlit · Streamlit Community Cloud · Kaggle US Airline Tweets dataset

## Overview

**#OOTD (Outfit of the Day)** is an AI personal stylist built around the user's own digital closet. It's designed to feel like a visual Pinterest/Instagram-style app rather than a generic chatbot.

> "What's the vibe? Let's build your OOTD. ✨"

## Key features

- **Digital closet**: add clothes, shoes, bags and accessories by pasting a store link (image, brand, colour and price are pulled in) or by uploading a photo that the AI identifies and tags.
- **AI styling engine**: generates complete outfits from what you actually own, considering colour and style compatibility, formality, occasion, weather and what you wore recently — and explains why the outfit works.
- **Occasion-based styling**: preset occasions (corporate, interviews, date night, weddings, brunch, gym and more) or any custom occasion in plain language.
- **Outfit cards**: save, wear, regenerate, or find similar inspiration.
- **Always-on stylist chat** available anywhere in the app.

## Built with

Gemini via Google AI Studio, synced to GitHub. Responsive across desktop, tablet and mobile.

## Planned

Pinterest inspiration matched against your closet ("you already own 4 pieces of this look"), outfit history grouped by occasion, repeat-outfit warnings, "last worn" reminders and preference learning.

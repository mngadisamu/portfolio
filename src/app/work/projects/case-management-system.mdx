## Overview

At MICT SETA, the business asked for a case management dashboard — but the requirement was vague. Rather than building to the brief as written, I went back to the people the system was for.

## What I did

- **Clarified the problem**: consulted other departments to understand how cases are actually raised, handled and reported on, and where the existing process broke down.
- **Redesigned the requirement**: reshaped the original dashboard request into a case management solution that matched real workflows.
- **Documented it for build**: user stories with positive and negative acceptance criteria, business rules, process maps and system flows.
- **Planned delivery**: built a sprint roadmap so the development team could deliver the system incrementally.

## Skills used

Requirements elicitation · Stakeholder consultation · User stories & acceptance criteria · Process mapping · Sprint planning

## Outcome

A clear, validated set of requirements and a delivery roadmap in place of an ambiguous one-line request.

## The challenge

Each group being onboarded needs different information and different checks, and every check has to be traceable for compliance. The platform had to handle all three journeys without becoming three separate systems, and plug into outside verification services.

## What I did

1. **Elicited and validated requirements** with the business for each onboarding journey.
2. **Mapped each journey** and the system flows between the platform and the verification services.
3. **Modelled the data** with ERDs for the people, documents and check results the platform needed to store.
4. **Wrote user stories with positive and negative acceptance criteria**, plus the business rules behind each check, so failure paths were as clear as happy paths.
5. **Designed wireframes and UI concepts** in Figma and Visily.
6. **Researched API integrations** so the team understood how third-party verification could connect.

## What I learned

Compliance-heavy systems live or die on the *negative* paths: what happens when a check fails, comes back incomplete, or the external service is down. Writing negative acceptance criteria for each check made those paths explicit before development, not after.

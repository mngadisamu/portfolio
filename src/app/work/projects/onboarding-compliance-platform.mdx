## Overview

**Timeframe:** 10 March 2025 – 27 February 2026 · MICT SETA

A single platform at MICT SETA for onboarding three very different groups — employees, suppliers/contractors, and beneficiaries — each with its own compliance and verification needs.

## Scope

- **Onboarding journeys** for employees, suppliers/contractors and beneficiaries.
- **Verification checks**: identity, KYC/AML, address, criminal, financial, citizenship and SAQA qualification checks.
- **HR features**: HR dashboards, leave management and PMDS (performance management).
- **Integrations**: researched the third-party APIs needed to run verification checks automatically.

## What I did

- Elicited and documented requirements with the business and validated them with stakeholders.
- Mapped each onboarding journey and the system flows between the platform and verification services.
- Built ERDs / data models for the people, documents and check results the platform needed to store.
- Wrote user stories with positive and negative acceptance criteria, and the business rules behind each check.
- Produced wireframes and UI concepts in Figma and Visily.

## Skills used

Requirements analysis · Process & system flow mapping · Data modelling · API integration research · Wireframing
